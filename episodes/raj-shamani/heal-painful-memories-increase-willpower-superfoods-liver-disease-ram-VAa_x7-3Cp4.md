## Important tidbits
- Ram Verma's core thesis: many chronic and autoimmune diseases are, in his view, driven by unprocessed painful memories, and he claims deleting or neutralising those memories through NLP techniques can reverse conditions including thyroid disorders, rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, type 1 diabetes and lupus.
- His signature "TV technique" for a painful memory: picture the memory playing on a TV behind glass, mute the sound repeatedly, drain it to black-and-white, freeze the frame, then erase it — he says this strips the memory of its emotional charge.
- He claims personal recovery, through these methods, from migraine, peptic ulcer, alopecia and rheumatoid arthritis.
- On willpower: he says willpower "does not exist" as a standalone force — behaviour is driven by pain and pleasure, and lasting change needs a strong, heartfelt "why" rather than discipline.
- Foods he tells people to quit: refined vegetable oils, refined sugar (he links it to advanced glycation end-products, AGEs), milk and milk products (he blames casein), and white salt — he recommends sea salt instead, claiming it carries 70+ minerals, and cites Dr Leonard Coldwell's suggestion of about one spoon of salt in 3 litres of water.
- His "superfoods" list: seasonal fruit, kanji (he suggests roughly half a litre daily), prebiotic acacia (babool) gum powder, flax and chia seeds, moringa, garlic and onion.
- Fibre targets he states: roughly 28–35 g per day for men and 25–28 g per day for women, framed around prebiotic fibre feeding gut bacteria.
- Gut concepts he walks through: SIBO/SIFO (small-intestinal bacterial/fungal overgrowth) and LPS endotoxins leaking from the gut as drivers of inflammation.
- Hygiene-hypothesis segment: he says he has not used soap for 1 year and 9 days, using a sea-salt water rub instead, and argues over-sanitising weakens the immune system.
- The episode also covers self-image as a driver of behaviour, and segments on telepathy and telekinesis, which he presents as real mental capacities.

## Claims to be careful with
- The central claim — that NLP memory techniques can cure autoimmune diseases including type 1 diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis and lupus — is the guest's personal assertion; it is not established medical treatment, and stopping prescribed medication for these conditions is dangerous.
- The claim that milk/casein causes osteoporosis and related diseases, and figures quoted around it, are presented without reliable sourcing and run against mainstream nutrition evidence.
- Describing wheat as a "poison" and invoking BT-toxin claims about modern wheat is not supported by mainstream food-safety evidence.
- Dr Leonard Coldwell, cited approvingly for salt-water intake and a claim of 30,000+ cancer cures, is a controversial figure whose cure claims are unverified.
- Claims about telepathy and telekinesis are presented as fact in the episode but have no scientific validation.
- Advice to avoid soap and to be relaxed about antibiotics/hygiene goes against standard medical guidance, particularly for people with infections or weakened immunity.

## Summary
NLP trainer Ram Verma argues that painful memories stored in the subconscious drive autoimmune and lifestyle disease, and demonstrates his "TV technique" for erasing a memory's emotional charge, alongside claims about his own recoveries. The second half is a gut-health programme: quit refined oil, refined sugar, milk and white salt; favour sea salt, seasonal fruit, kanji, acacia gum, seeds, moringa and garlic; and hit daily prebiotic-fibre targets — all framed by his view that willpower is a myth and only a heartfelt "why" changes behaviour.
