## Important tidbits
- Dr Krishna says cancer almost always gives an early sign: a breast lump with pricking pain, alternating diarrhoea and constipation for colon cancer, unexplained bloating for liver cancer, and spotting for cervical cancer.
- He frames cancer as an immunity failure (macrophages and T-helper cells), noting that TLC and ESR rise on a CBC blood report when the body is fighting something.
- His CANCER acronym for prevention: Cut alcohol and smoking, Active lifestyle, Nutrition-rich food, Checkups regularly, Exposure to sunlight, Rest well.
- Annual tests he lists: CBC, LFT, KFT, thyroid, extended lipid profile, CA 15.3 and CA 125 for women, CEA, CA 72.4 (stomach/colon), PSA for men after 40, and a whole-abdomen ultrasound (about ₹1,000).
- His SMILE acronym: Sleep, Move 10,000–12,000 steps, Immunity-boosting foods, Love/support, Exposure to sunlight.
- On vitamin D: he claims roughly 90% of Indians are deficient and recommends maximum sun exposure between 9 AM and 12 PM with skin exposed.
- He explains cancer stages 1–4 in plain terms and gives a treatment-goals ladder: quality of life → longevity → stability → regression → cure.
- His "triad of longevity": willpower 33%, support system 33%, correct treatment 33%.
- He argues suppressed emotions and toxic environments raise cancer risk, recommending journaling ("emotional vomit"), venting to a trusted circle, and a sandpaper grounding trick he attributes to UK therapists.
- Lifestyle patterns he endorses from Blue Zones/Okinawa: community living and hara hachi bu — stop eating at 80% full.
- On diets: he says intermittent fasting helps, but warns against a ketogenic diet unless gene expression supports it.
- Home remedies he rates as useful: tulsi, giloy, wheatgrass juice (for blood cancers), ashwagandha, aloe vera juice, papaya leaf extract (for platelets), and neem (capsules preferred); his immunity shot is raw turmeric + ginger + amla + black pepper. He says turmeric is good but milk is bad in turmeric milk.
- Household exposures he flags: microwaving food in plastic and BPA leaching, plastic water bottles, burnt roti/meat (acrylamide), aluminium utensils, SLS toothpaste, titanium dioxide, formaldehyde in nail polish, and phthalates/parabens in shampoo. His top three changes: remove aluminium cookware, switch to SLS-free toothpaste, and replace plastic bottles with glass, steel, or copper.
- Sleep: he advises sleeping by 10 PM for a 10 PM–2 AM melatonin/DNA-repair window, fixing wake time to reset circadian rhythm, no coffee after 4 PM, and avoiding external melatonin. He also advises changing pillows at least yearly (VOC absorption) and toothbrushes every 6 months.

## Claims to be careful with
- The statement that loneliness increases cancer risk "as much as smoking 15 cigarettes a day" was cited by the host from research and endorsed by the guest; the underlying study is about mortality risk generally, and the cancer-specific framing should be treated cautiously.
- Claims that home remedies such as wheatgrass juice help blood cancers, and that papaya leaf extract raises platelets, are the guest's clinical opinions, not established treatments.
- The 10 PM–2 AM "DNA-repair window," pillow VOC, and "aluminium cookware is the #1 change" claims are presented without cited evidence.
- The guest runs a commercial cancer-healing practice; his framing of immunity-based treatment alongside conventional oncology should not be read as a substitute for oncologist-led care.

## Summary
Dr Tarang Krishna of the Cancer Healer Center lays out an immunity-centred view of cancer: watch for early signs, test annually, and follow his CANCER and SMILE frameworks covering food, movement, sleep, sunlight, and emotional health. He also warns against everyday chemical exposures — aluminium cookware, plastics, SLS toothpaste — and rates several Ayurvedic home remedies he uses with patients.
