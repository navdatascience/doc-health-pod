# Health Tidbits — the daily podcast

A daily 4–5 minute podcast distilled from the [Doctor Health Podcast Library](../README.md) in this repo. Every episode takes one collected conversation — a doctor or health expert from the Dr Pal playlist, Raj Shamani, or The Diary Of A CEO — and turns its most important tidbits into a short, listenable briefing.

- **Hosts:** Mimi and Alex
- **Cadence:** one episode daily
- **Feed:** [feed.xml](feed.xml) (RSS — point any podcast app at the raw GitHub URL of that file)
- **Cover:** [cover.webp](cover.webp)
