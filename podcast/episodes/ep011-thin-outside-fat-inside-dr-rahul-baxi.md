## What this episode covers

- India's diabetes scale per Dr Baxi: about 90 million people with diabetes (roughly 10-11% of the population, about 1 in 10 in a room), plus roughly 40-50 million with prediabetes, and 40-50% of people with diabetes in India unaware they have it.
- South Asian risk: being South Asian is itself a major risk factor, and the TOFI ("thin outside, fat inside") hypothesis — smaller fat cells overflow sooner, spilling visceral fat around the liver and gut, so a person can look thin at BMI 25, or even 22-23, yet have fatty liver, high triglycerides and diabetes.
- Myth 1: "sugar-free" biscuits are not healthy — the first ingredient is refined flour (maida) and the second is palm oil, and patients often eat more of them believing they are good for diabetes.
- Myth 2: fruit juice, even without added sugar — a glass takes 4-5 oranges, discards the fibre, and on CGM spikes blood sugar within 20-40 minutes; whole fruit is the better form, eaten as a mid-meal snack in the right portion.
- The flipped plate: 40-50% fibre and vegetables, plenty of protein, only a small portion of carbohydrate, adapted to culture (4 idlis cut to 2 plus protein) rather than crash keto, which most people quit.
- Catching damage early: yearly urine microalbumin-creatinine ratio (early kidney damage can hide behind normal creatinine) and a separate yearly retina examination; late osmotic symptoms mean waiting too long.
- A post-meal walk is "like medicine, like insulin" and free: starting 20-30 minutes after eating blunts the spike, and even 7-10 minutes helps.
- Caution: the headline numbers were stated in conversation without the underlying reports named, and the TOFI / small-adipocyte explanation is a hypothesis under study, not a proven sole cause.

Where the childhood plate was half carbohydrate, he now wants forty to fifty percent fibre and vegetables, plenty of protein, and only a small portion of carbohydrate.

The warning signs of very high sugar, like waking three or four times a night to urinate, intense thirst and weight loss, often arrive only late, after years of damage. Eyes need a separate yearly retina examination too, not just a glasses check.
